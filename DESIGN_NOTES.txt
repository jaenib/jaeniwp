Design decisions for jaenib.com/cv — reworked July 2026

Source of truth
- The site is a 1:1 web translation of the 2026 print portfolio
  (01_JB_portfolio_26.pdf, 49 pages). Content and aesthetics follow the book.
- Figures in cv/assets/portfolio/ are rendered straight from the PDF
  (named <project>-<printed page>-<n>.jpg). Narrative text was redacted from
  drawing pages before rendering so copy lives in HTML, not baked into images.

Palette and form
- White ground (#fff), near-black ink (#111), soft gray-black for captions.
- Dark full-bleed bands (#0d0c0a) reproduce the book's black spreads
  (Kanzlei models/ghosts, Invisibility LiDAR pages, Chandoline drawing).
- No gradients, no rounded corners, no glass effects. Print-like restraint.
- Swiss grotesque system stack: "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial. No webfonts.
- Titles: bold uppercase, tight leading, like the book's project openers.
- Captions: italic, 0.8rem. Section heads carry the ■ square from the CV page.
- Narrow justified text columns with hyphenation mirror the book's setting.

Structure
- cv/index.html         cover + Motivation + Selected Projects TOC + contact.
- cv/lebenslauf.html    two-column German CV, mirrors book page 1.
- cv/projects/*.html    one page per project, book order, prev/next in footer:
  binz, kanzlei, invisibility, storey-vss, semantic-similarity,
  sensing-seebach, dream-together, bkf-dietikon, k118.
- Footers show the printed page range (pp. x–y) as a nod to the book.
- cv/jan-bauer-portfolio.pdf: 100dpi Ghostscript build of the book WITHOUT
  page 1 (the CV page carries street address and phone — never publish those).

Content policy (unchanged)
- Do not publish street address or phone number anywhere, including PDFs.
- Public contact is jan.ba.jb@gmail.com until changed.
- Keep the PDF download sanitized and aligned with on-page content.
- Videos stay short, muted-by-default mp4s in cv/assets/video/.

Regeneration
- Figure extraction scripts lived in the session scratchpad; approach:
  PyMuPDF render of per-figure page regions at ~1700–2400px wide, JPEG q85
  (q72 re-pass for files >600KB), text blocks redacted on drawing pages,
  autocrop within a 35pt inset to dodge the printer crop marks.

Future instructions
- If new projects are added, follow the book: title, meta block, narrow
  justified copy, figure rows with italic captions, dark band if the spread
  is dark in print.
- Do not reintroduce accent colors, glows, or UI chrome; the work is the color.
