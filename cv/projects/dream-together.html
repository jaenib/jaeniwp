Dream Together

2021
Tibilisi Architecture Biennal 2020
Panta Rhei Collaborative

with Benedikt Wahlbrink, Eugenio Capuccio

Dream Together is a contribution which me and collaborators from the Panta Rhei Collaborative were invited to develop for the Tibilisi Architecture Biennal 2020.

The Kartli Sanatorium is a squat outside Georgias capital. The recent displacement of IDPs from Abkhazia who have lived there for more than 30 years was adressed in the wider context of the biennal.

We conceived an interface where the inhabitants were able to communicate their minds. We then visualised the answers using open source image generation technology.

The result was exhibited at the biennal where we discussed the role of generative AI in the public discourse of the built environment and its potential to amplify the voices of those untrained in visualisation, especially in visual subjects such as the built environment.
