Familiarity on Kanzlei

2022
Studio Adam Caruso
Assistant Tibor Bielicky

With Max Schubert

Looking once again for new battlegrounds to fight the housing crisis, the stage is the Kanzleiareal.

Next to cinema, club and school it frequently hosts political activism, non-institutional market and trades, pétanque and many more invisible appropriations.

The Non-Complete is a stance opposing the legitimacy of power structures, suspicious of stability, unity, and the established. It suggests constant change, with shifting, sometimes conflicting, translations to reality.

To build on the Kanzleiareal means to introduce a stranger.

The faminiar stranger: For each side we read the atmosphere already present and contribute by deploying suitable elements and principles from our catalogue.
